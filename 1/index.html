When closer up and zooming out, the nose takes up more of the frame because the relative distance between the facial features and the camera is much smaller. However, moving farther away from the camera means the relative proportion between the features of the face is much smaller, so they are rendered as more evenly sized.

This is similar to the previous example. When closer, the relative distance between the camera and the different corners of the building is more different compared to when standing farther away, where the distance of the different corners of the buildings is more similar.
